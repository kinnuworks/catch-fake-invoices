# InvoiceGuard

Tagline: Catch fake invoices before paying.

Design system: the indie app design system, `mist` mood with the `amber` accent. Tokens and base components come from its foundation CSS, in `app/app/globals.css`.

The page looks like the app it sells: a dark product window on a colourful gradient desk, on a white and soft-grey page. Amber is the only brand accent, and it fills the main button, the logo and active states. Red and green are product colours, used only for results inside the app window.

Signature: the audit line. A red line draws under the field that changed, so the mark points at the evidence. For a PDF, hidden text appears in red on its own panel. The logo is the same idea: lines of an invoice, with the last one underlined in red.

## Page order

Floating nav, hero with the live demo, why it matters, PDF check, how it works, questions, final call to action, footer. Pricing, testimonials and logos are left out because there are none to show yet.
